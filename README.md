# Quality Control Inspection Study

A short online study on how people make decisions during quality control inspection. It is part of a University of Missouri industrial and systems engineering research project (ISE 3810).

**[Start the study](YOUR-GITHUB-PAGES-LINK)**

## About the researcher

My name is Sam Herbert. I am the ESS, Inc. Health & Safety Intern and an industrial engineering student at the University of Missouri.

## What you will do

- Answer a few short background questions.
- Review a series of manufactured parts and decide whether each should **pass** or **fail** inspection.
- Rate your confidence along the way and answer a brief survey at the end.

The study takes about 20 minutes. It works on a phone, tablet, or computer, and you do not need an account or login.

## Participation and privacy

- Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.
- You must be 18 or older.
- Your responses are anonymous and will be used only for an academic research project.
- At the end, you may add a name, questions, or comments. These are optional, are saved separately from your answers, and are not linked to them.
